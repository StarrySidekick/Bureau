# Reviewing a bench

Score each bench on these, one line each, then name the two or three changes
that would matter most. Look at it in WebKit first (`test/safari.mjs` shots, or
a scratch script that makes the bench and screenshots every tile).

| # | Question | A good answer | A bad sign |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The one thing.** Can its purpose be said in one sentence, in Timothy's words? | "Everything out of your head, sorted later" | two activities on one bench |
| 2 | **The first screen.** Is what matters in the first screenful, top left first? | the work object is the biggest thing | instructions or links take the best spot |
| 3 | **Room to breathe.** Does each tile hold one moment? | a few things with space between | many things at 2 cells wide, a title cut to fit (*cramped*) |
| 4 | **Where am I.** Can you tell which tile you are on and what else there is? | one tile, or a carousel you can feel the edges of | a swipe that slides into empty wood, nothing saying a tile goes on |
| 5 | **The motion fits.** Does the scroll type match the moments (§ patterns)? | rigid between stages, smooth down a list | rigid on one tile; a scroll on something that should hold still |
| 6 | **Reach.** Are the verbs done everywhere one press away in the front? | the stamp on a bench where stamping is the decision | a tool the bench never uses (the coin on a bench for emptying a head) |
| 7 | **The way in.** Is there a line to write into, writing to the right place? | a notepad writing `into` the list it feeds | a line that drops things loose on the board |
| 8 | **The way out.** Does it send you to where the work is done? | a link to the app | nothing, or five links |
| 9 | **Decisions as placement.** Is a choice a drop, a stamp or a swipe? | zones, a stamp a sorting drawer collects, a ranking deck | a choice that needs the editor |
| 10 | **The room.** Does the aesthetic suit the activity, and is gravity off unless it is the point? | a screening room for film | the desk's look, by accident |
| 11 | **Reusable.** Is everything in it a Bureau feature another bench could use? | yes, and the gaps are in the ledger | something only this bench can do |

The full review of the seven built benches, through the navigation patterns,
is `docs/NAVIGATION.md` §7. Repeat it when a bench changes, and update that
section rather than writing a second one.
