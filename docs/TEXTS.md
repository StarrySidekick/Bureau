# Texts

What a note, a poem, a letter or a script made at random is born holding (decision 244): `web/data/texts.json`. Every text is public domain in the United States and was fetched from the source below, not typed from memory. Italics are Markdown `*x*`; Gutenberg's `--` is an em dash; drop capitals are ordinary case; editorial notes, line numbers and footnote markers are removed. Blake's "The Tyger" keeps his own engraved spelling and punctuation.
