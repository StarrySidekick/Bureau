## Functions
1. **Self-Expression** - Outwardly recording at-the-time emotions without a filter

> Open, unstructured environment to convey emotions as you please. Should be distanced from technical systems, or at least not require any technical constraints within the creative space.

* Daily Journaling
* Freewriting
* Drawing/Sketching
* Musical Doodling
* Visual Customization

Paper Type - Physical
Routine - Daily
Structure - Low
Recreational

**Physical**	-	Ample amounts of blank\dotted paper with a diverse marker\pencil set and easily accessible 
				Medium-Sized Journal with a portable “supplies box” and durable paper
Bureau		-	Interface options, titling, naming, custom systems, formatting, colors
Bear		-	GOOD, Journaling, Free Writing, tagcons, themes, custom tags, more options

2. **Early-Stage Ideation (Brainstorming)** - Inventing brand new ideas or developing something in it’s early stages

> Large, unstructured space with very VERY low barrier to entry and almost no setup. Essential that there is a “anything goes” mentality shift. Sometimes a “starter” may be needed, like a *constraint* *catalyst* *template* *problem* or *question*. Often part of the first steps in doing an open-ended activity like drawing or free-writing involve brainstorming what to do, and an activity template can be created with catalysts to help kickstart this process. Can also be repeated as necessary within the development of a project to fulfill the need for more specific ideas.

Paper Type	- 	Physical
Routine		- 	On Demand
Structure	- 	None (Besides Starter)
Professional

**Physical**	-	Large erasable space, room to pace, easily accessible, multiple writing colors
				Large Whiteboard with Dry-Erase Set
				Midori Journal with Bulleted Insert and Pen Pocket
Bureau		-	Color selection, custom lists, filtering and grouping, idea item
Bear		-	GOOD, Idea lists, quick note creation, custom tagging, low structure

4. **Late-Stage Ideation (Development)** - Actually building the idea, and the paper structure that supports it

> Heavy usage of tagging\grouping\linking along with maintaining a back catalog of information that is easy to access, maintain flexibility and be regularly added upon. Also should feature template and outline functionality. Needs to support the ebb and flow of the process of creation, along with enough wiggle room to accommodate a pivot or restructuring. Also in the case of groups need to support collaboration and sharing of information (this might need its own function).

Paper Type	- 	Digital
Routine 		- 	On Demand
Structure	-	High
Professional

Physical		-	Project timelines, printable itineraries, post-it reminder systems, team-based workshopping
				Good Thick Paper
				Post It Notes
				Large Whiteboard
**Bureau**	-	Robust cataloging, project templates, lists and tables, bulletins
Bear		-	OK, Tagging, lists

5. **Documentation** - Recording events so they are not lost to time

> Should be very easy to record information and habitual to the extent that it is second nature. Needs a memorized underlying structure in order to pull out the events of the day as fast and in as much important detail as possible.

* Daily Logging
* Life at a Glance
* Queueing

Paper Type - Digital
Routine - Constant (Daily Otherwise)
Structure - Moderate
Personal

Physical		-	Memory chests, portfolios, journaling
				Good Looking Chest
				Media Folders
				Midori Journal
**Bureau**	-	Happenings, cataloging system, filters, timestamping
Bear		-	OK, Queue tag system, lists

6. **Reflection** - Attempting to understand, recap, and digest past events in order to learn and grow.

> Easy of access, habitual and structured in a way to get you looking at your day/experiences in as much detail as possible.

Paper Type - Both
Routine - Daily -> Weekly -> Monthly
Structure - Moderate
Personal

**Physical**	-	Viewing old journals and periodic general entries, posting stuff on walls\tables
				Midori Journal with Blank Bleed-Proof Insert
				Sticky Notes
				All-Inclusive Wall Calendar *maybe* 
**Bureau**	-	“Accordion” formatting, the timeline, pattern logging, Happenings, Drawers
Bear		-	BAD, Archive, sort by date modified, some types of user tagging

7. **Queuing/Focus Topics** - A method in which a topic of thought is forced and all excess information is “queued”

> Completely distraction free, and easily integrated into other paper systems so that the information is cataloged automatically, reducing busywork. Sometimes needs a physical “statement space” to be effective, based on my experience.

Paper Type - Digital
Routine - On Demand
Structure - Moderate
Universal


Physical		-	Journal, only used as a backup
**Bureau**	-	Global timeline, ability to convert reserved topic into notes
Bear		-	OK, Queue within to-do list


8. **Cataloging** - Filtering and organizing all pieces of information deemed valuable from any source.

> Heavy use of filters, tagging, and categorization, with a focus on organizing information quickly in order to create a sort of “database”. Similar to the idea of a “Personal Wikipedia Page”.

Paper Type - Digital
Routine - On Demand
Structure - Very High
Universal

Physical		-	Room\Workspace organization, clothing
**Bureau**	-	Drawers, Item differentiation, auto-sorting, filters
Bear		-	BAD, Tagging, manually sorting queue content

9. **Notetaking** - Recording a summarizing information gained from an outside source.

> Format should support abridging and summarizing information as easily as possible. Unique in that there is a focus on memory forming, and so benefits from physical/spacial environments. Paired with the mental direction stage of “Observation”.

Paper Type - Digital -> Physical
Routine - On Demand
Structure - Moderate -> None
Professional

**Physical**	-	Large durable contained blank paper surface to craft succinct notes
				Cardstock or Thick Sketch Paper
				Colored Writing Material
**Bureau**	-	Sections for each type of note, quick access, list and outline functionality, colors
Bear		-	GOOD, Pursuit Tags, clear note syntax
